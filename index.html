Velocity–Impulse Incompressible Flow Solver

Developing an in-house incompressible CFD framework to compare E–Liu impulse-density and geometric impulse formulations under a common numerical architecture, with emphasis on projection methods, stability, validation, and GPU acceleration.

  • Implemented advection, diffusion, scalar-potential/Poisson coupling, face-consistent velocity recovery, and RK4 projection at intermediate stages.
  • Compared formulation behavior through lid-driven-cavity and Taylor–Green benchmarks, grid refinement, convergence metrics, and long-time stability studies.
  • Progressed the code from CPU SOR baselines to CUDA projection solvers and higher-resolution 2D/3D research branches.
  • Current turbulence-modeling branches are intentionally private until the associated research results are published.
Engineering question

How do mathematically related velocity–impulse formulations differ in numerical stability, grid sensitivity, and benchmark accuracy when implemented in the same incompressible-flow framework?

Numerical workflow

Advance impulse variables → solve the scalar-potential constraint → recover divergence-free velocity → evaluate the next RK stage → monitor residuals, divergence, and steady-state behavior.

Verification & validation

Ghia centerline comparisons, 128²/256²/512² mesh studies, Taylor–Green analytical validation, CFL control, Poisson residual tracking, and performance profiling.

Truncated Linear Aerospike Nozzle

Carried an aerospike concept through analytical contour generation, CAD, CFD, structural analysis, additive manufacturing, and cold-flow testing.

  • Modified a Python Method of Characteristics workflow to generate the linear-plug contour and developed a ~40% truncated design.
  • Used ANSYS Fluent to investigate Mach/pressure behavior, truncation, base pressure, and bleed-slot concepts before prototyping.
  • Used ANSYS FEA to evaluate aerospike-tip deformation and von Mises stress against Ti-6Al-4V material limits.

Engineering question

Can a shorter truncated aerospike preserve useful supersonic expansion behavior while remaining practical to manufacture, mount, and test?

Workflow

MOC contour → SolidWorks geometry → CFD / base-bleed study → structural checks → 3D printing → cold-flow prototype test.

Engineering value

A full design loop connecting analytical gas dynamics to geometry, simulation, structural decisions, manufacturing, and hardware testing.

Design value

RESEARCH

Numerical methods for incompressible flow.

WORLD-FIRST RESEARCH TARGET Developing what is intended to be the world’s first turbulence-capable velocity–impulse CFD solver.

Graduate research exploring impulse-based formulations as an alternative representation of incompressible momentum dynamics.

E–LIU FORMULATION
∂M/∂t + (u · ∇)u − ν∇²M = 0

Advection is evaluated from the recovered physical velocity, while viscous diffusion acts on the impulse field.

GEOMETRIC FORMULATION
∂M/∂t + (u · ∇)M − M · (∇u)ᵀ − ν∇²M = 0

The impulse itself is transported and coupled to the velocity-gradient tensor through the geometric stretching term.

Current focus

Velocity–impulse formulations, projection methods, pressure recovery, numerical stability, and GPU performance, with a turbulence-model-friendly solver architecture for high-Reynolds-number research. Current development also explores an offline-trained neural-network φ predictor that can provide a fast initial guess for the scalar-potential projection before the classical Poisson solver performs the final physics-enforcing correction. Turbulence-modeling source remains private until the associated results are published.
