ABOUT

Engineering across simulation, design, and computation.

I am a mechanical engineer working at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, numerical methods, and product development. My graduate research centers on an incompressible velocity–impulse formulation for high-Reynolds-number flow, including GPU acceleration, pressure/velocity recovery, turbulence modeling, and validation against canonical cavity-flow data.

Beyond solver development, I have worked on turbomachinery, aerospike nozzle design, centrifugal pump optimization, thermal systems, GD&T, machining, CAM/CNC workflows, 3D printing, and mechatronics prototypes.

Graduate Research2025–2026

GPU-Accelerated Velocity–Impulse Flow Solver

Developed a collocated incompressible flow solver using velocity–impulse variables, Poisson-based recovery, RK time integration, and GPU acceleration. Extended the framework toward high-Reynolds-number 3D cavity flow and turbulence modeling.

  • Python / Numba CUDA implementation with GPU-accelerated kernels.
  • Investigated EL and geometric impulse formulations, projection accuracy, numerical dissipation, and pressure recovery.
  • Validation against canonical lid-driven cavity centerline velocity data.
  • 3D LES / turbulence-model experiments up to Re ≈ 100,000.

Truncated Linear Aerospike Nozzle

Led simulation work for a 3D truncated linear plug nozzle, combining method-of-characteristics design, CAD, FEA, additive manufacturing, and cold-flow testing.

  • Designed a ~40% truncated spike geometry and supporting structure.
  • Used ANSYS for flow/structural studies and SolidWorks for mechanical integration.
  • Built and tested a 3D-printed prototype.

Centrifugal Pump Optimization

Evaluated blade-count and exit-angle effects on pump performance using 2D/3D CFD, while developing the impeller as a manufacturable engineering component.

  • Compared 5-, 7-, and 9-blade configurations at Re ≈ 105.
  • Designed and GD&T-d the impeller for a product-development course.
  • Integrated CAD, TurboGrid meshing, and ANSYS turbomachinery workflows.
